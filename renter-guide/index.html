Renter Guide

Welcome to Cape Cod Village Club, a community of 33 member families on Lake George. This guide is for guests who have already arranged a stay with a Club homeowner; the Club does not list, book or manage rentals. A few simple rules keep the Club safe and quiet for everyone. Please read this before you arrive and share it with everyone in your party.

Before you arrive

Register with the Club at least a week before your stay. Your homeowner will send you a link to register with the Club online. It takes about 10 minutes. Have these ready:

  • names and contact details for the person renting
  • your arrival and departure dates and the number of overnight guests
  • the make, model and license plate of each car (or “rental car”)
  • a copy of your renter’s insurance (PDF or photo)

The complete Club rules are shown, and signed, during registration, and a copy of your completed packet is emailed to you.

Bringing a boat? Start by getting your homeowner’s approval. Then request it from the Club as part of your registration, including the boat’s details and insurance. If you’re bringing a boat, register with the HOA at least three weeks before you arrive. The earlier, the better the chance space is available.

During your stay

The Club does not have a management company or front desk. The homeowner you are renting from is your primary point of contact for anything about your stay.

Parking

  • 10 mph speed limit.
  • Park only in the designated areas. Please keep cars and trailers off the lawns and off the edges of the roads.
  • No RVs or campers.

The beach and swimming

  • Swim only between the designated swim lines.
  • Children under 12 must be supervised by an adult at all times.
  • No glass and no alcohol on the beach or docks.

The playground

  • Children under 10 must be supervised by an adult at all times.
  • The playground closes at dark.

Boats and docks

  • Keep your boat in your house’s designated slip, unless other arrangements have been made with the Dockmaster.
  • No boats in the swim area. No diving off the docks.
  • All boats and kayaks on Club grounds are privately owned and are not for renters’ use.

Around the Club

  • Quiet hours begin at 9:30 pm.
  • No fireworks, weapons, or loud parties.
  • No pets. Service animals are allowed.

A full list of rules can be found in the registration packet.

Trash and recycling

Trash and recycling go in the dumpster on Buttner Lane, next to the Club workshop on the west side of Route 9N.

Who to contact

  • Emergencies: call 911.
  • Questions about the house or your stay: contact the homeowner you are renting from. Each home is different, and your homeowner is your primary point of contact.
  • The Club: contactus@capecodvillageclub.com

Before you leave

Please put away beach and playground equipment, and leave the docks and beach as you found them.

We’re glad you’re here. Enjoy the lake.
